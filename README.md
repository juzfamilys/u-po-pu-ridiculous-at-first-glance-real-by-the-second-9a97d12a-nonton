# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

Entry for the [make.fun competition "Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second"](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a): one sentence (max 200 characters) pitching a bizarre startup idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## The pitch

> We sell funerals for dead houseplants: grieving owners pay $29 for a tiny coffin, a eulogy, and a replacement plant that ships with a 'do better' care plan.

(156 characters, one sentence.)

Why it works: the buyer is obvious (millions of people who keep killing houseplants and feel guilty about it), the price is concrete ($29), and the business holds together — the funeral is the hook, but the replacement plant plus care plan is the repeat revenue. Ridiculous at first glance, real by the second.

## How it runs

`main.py` (pure Python standard library, no dependencies) writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
